Pitch Colorizer by Aquanode

Pitch Colorizer is a simple remake of Pitch Colorization tools like Pitchmap from Zynaptiq.
This FL Studio Patcher Plugin works by chaining many Fruity Parametric EQ 2s in parallel such that
over all accessible octaves all note frequencies that are in key are increased by 24 dB and
all notes not in key are reduced in volume by 24 dB.

This is a small "proof of concept" that you can achieve similar sounding results to much more
sophisticated programs only using a couple of EQs with very narrow peak bands.
The routing inside FL Studio's Patcher might look a little complicated since I automated EQ band access
a little, but in its core it is a simple patch.
