/////// AutoMorph EQs by Aquanode

... is a collection of Presets for FL Studio's Patcher Plugin that use 
Fruity Parametric EQ Automations to create some interesting Filter Effects
inspired by Morph EQ from Minimal Audio and E-MU's z-Plane Filter Technology,
Zynaptiq's Pitchmap Pitch Colorization Techniques and more unique ideas!

Offered for free on aquanode.gumroad.com.
