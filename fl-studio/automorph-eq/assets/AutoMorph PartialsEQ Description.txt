AutoMorph Partials EQ is a simple EQ with which you can dial in the frequency of a note and then
either attenuate or amplify up to 14 of its partials (harmonics), where the volume change for the 1st
partial can controlled individually. It is simple, since it does not snap to notes (you can continuously
increase or decrease the frequency) and it is static (you'd have to automate the frequency knob
if you want it to follow a note progression). Nonetheless it is a unique "EQ Exciter" kind of effect, 
and also has a pseudo-randomization knob for interesting volume change of the partials.

Controls:
- Volume: Controls the volume of the partials. They roll off a little by default except at 100% volume.
- Frequency: To manually dial in the frequency (note) of the playing sound.
- 1st Partial: Control the volume of the fundamental note. This overwrites the setting of the volume,
	       however changing the volume will also change the 1st partial note so you have to turn
	       the 1st Partial knob a little again after changing the volume if you want to keep the
	       1st Partial at a different volume.
- Width: The bandwidth of all the EQ bands. Since high bandwidth values overlap quickly, Partials EQ
         has a built-in Limiter at the end of its signal chain to avoid extremely loud volumes.
- RandAmp: Turning this knob will pseudo-randomly change the volume of each of the partials (except the
  	   root / 1st partial). Automating this knob can also sound interesting (right-click -> create 
	   automation clip).
