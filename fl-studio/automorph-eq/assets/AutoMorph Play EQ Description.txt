///////// AutoMorph Play EQ 
//// Midi Note Controllable Bandpass Equalizer Preset for FL Studio's Patcher
// by Aquanode (aquanode.bandcamp.com, aquanode.gumroad.com)

With AutoMorph Play EQ you can play bandpass filters tuned to note frequencies with your Midi Keyboard as if the EQ was a synthesizer. 
For this you need a sound source that is already playing (e.g. your track in song mode) on some mixer track where you want to put Play EQ. 
For playing the EQ you need to go to to your synth tracks and open FL Studio's "MIDI Out" synth, which then automatically controls the EQ,
since Play EQ is set to receive Midi notes from Midi Channel 0 (the default in MIDI Out). 
You can play all notes from C1 to B9 without polyphony limit and the bands are velocity sensitive (if you have a Midi Keyboard that has that function).

Parameters:
- Attack: Sets the time it takes for the EQ note to reach peak velocity.
- Release: Sets the time it takes for the EQ to turn back to 0dB after releasing a key.
- Bandwidth: Sets the bandwidth of the bandpass filter centered around a note's pitch. For higher bandwidths I recommend the +12dB setting.
- +12dB / +24dB: Sets the highest possible velocity amplification to either +12 or +24dB when a key is pressed with 100% velocity.
- Wet Only: If Wet Only is activated, the original input sound is turned off by phase cancelling it with an inverted copy of the changed signal.
            This will only let the changed frequencies corresponding to the pressed notes through.
