///////// AutoMorph Spread EQ 
//// 7 Band Filter Peak Spreader Preset for FL Studio's Patcher
// by Aquanode (aquanode.bandcamp.com, aquanode.gumroad.com)

AutoMorph Spread EQ is a Phaser-like Preset with equidistant Peak Spreading Function.

How to use:
Volume:		Defines the peak volume of the bands (-12 to +12 dB).
Center Freq.:	Defines the center frequency around which the bands are dispersed. Band 4 will stay at this frequency.
Spread:		The frequency range between two band peaks.
Wobble: 	Introduces some slight frequency wobble effect if activated.
